# House Rules

The short version: be a good neighbour. Everyone here is building something.

## Access
- **One access card per seat.** Cards are personal and must not be shared or lent.
- **No tailgating:** don't hold the door for people you don't know.
- **Lost card:** report it to reception or support@ straight away so we can deactivate it. A replacement costs ₹500.
- **Hours:**
  - Hot desk members and day-pass users: 8 am–9 pm, Monday to Saturday.
  - Dedicated desk, cabin and Founders Plan members: 24×7.
  - Hot desk members can add 24×7 access for ₹1,500/month.

## Guests and visitors
- **Registering visitors:** register them in the member app or at reception. Visitors get a visitor badge and must be accompanied.
- **Meeting visitors:** guests coming for a meeting (in a meeting room, cabin or the café) are **free**.
- **Guests working at a desk** for the day need a **guest day pass at ₹400**, billed to the member.
- **Limits:** at most **4 visitors per member at a time** in common areas. For bigger groups, book a meeting room or the event hall.
- **After hours:** after 9 pm, visitors must be pre-registered and must leave by 11 pm.

## Pets 🐾
- **Pets are allowed only at HSR Layout**, in the ground-floor **Paws Zone** (open-desk area and courtyard), Monday to Friday.
- **Conditions:**
  - Dogs only, with up-to-date vaccinations. Upload the vaccination card in the app once.
  - Dogs stay on a leash in shared areas.
  - The owner signs a pet waiver.
  - Maximum 6 dogs on site at a time; book a slot in the app.
- **No pets at Koramangala, Indiranagar, Whitefield or Hebbal**, except assistance animals.
- Members at other centres can use the Paws Zone at HSR with a guest day pass (₹400, or one of their hot-desk days).

## Noise and calls
- Use **phone booths** for calls longer than a few minutes. If others are waiting, booths are limited to 30 minutes.
- No speakerphone calls in open areas. Wear headphones for video calls.
- **Quiet zones** (marked in blue) are for silent work only.

## Food and drink
- Unlimited tea, coffee and filtered water in every pantry.
- No cooking. Microwaves are available.
- Eat strong-smelling food in the café or pantry, not at open desks.
- **Alcohol only at approved events.** **No smoking or vaping** anywhere inside the building. Use the designated outdoor areas.

## Mail and couriers
- Reception accepts mail and couriers for members **9 am–7 pm, Monday to Saturday**. We notify you in the app.
- **Uncollected items** are held for **7 days**, then returned to the sender.
- **Large deliveries (over 20 kg, or furniture or equipment):** tell reception at least 1 business day ahead. They come through the service lift.
- **We do not accept** cash-on-delivery parcels, perishables, or hazardous items.
- **Virtual-office members:** mail is scanned on request and physically forwarded once a fortnight. Courier charges are billed at cost.

## Events
- **Members may host events** in the event halls (KOR, WHF) or in meeting rooms. Book through sales@ or the app.
- **Community events** (Demo Night, office hours, workshops) are free for members.
- **Events for more than 20 external guests** need 7 days' notice and a guest list.

## Workspace care
- Don't move furniture between zones. Ask the community team instead.
- **Clear desk policy for hot desks:** take your belongings with you at the end of each day. Items left overnight go to lost and found.
- Cabins can be personalised with posters and whiteboards. Nothing may be drilled into or stuck to the walls without the centre manager's approval.

## Network and IT
- **Member Wi-Fi** ("Launchbench") is separate from **guest Wi-Fi** ("Launchbench-Guest").
- Cabins can request a **private VLAN (free)** or a **static public IP (₹1,500/month)**.
- **Not allowed:** running servers that accept inbound traffic, crypto mining, or bandwidth-heavy torrenting on the shared network.

## Safety
- Know your nearest fire exit; floor plans are posted near the lifts.
- **Fire drills** happen every quarter and everyone must take part.
- **Report safety hazards immediately.** They are always treated as P1 (see [05-facility-sla.md](05-facility-sla.md)).

## Conduct
- Launchbench has **zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination**. Report any incident confidentially to the centre manager or the COO.
- No sales canvassing or door-to-door pitching to other members. Use the community board in the app instead.
