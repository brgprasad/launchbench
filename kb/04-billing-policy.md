# Billing Policy

Questions about any of this go to **teamlaunchbench@gmail.com**, owned by Finance Manager **Lakshmi Narayan**. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 am–6:30 pm. We reply within **1 business day**.

## 1. Invoice cycle
- **Invoices are issued on the 1st of every month** and sent by email to the billing contact. Copies are also in the member app ("Launchpad").
- **Invoice numbers** use the format `LB/<FY>/<sequence>`, for example `LB/26-27/0412`. FY 26-27 runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- **What each invoice contains:**
  - **Seat fees** for the current month, **billed in advance**.
  - **Add-ons** for the current month: parking, lockers, 24×7 access, static IP.
  - **Usage** from the **previous month, billed in arrears**: meeting-room overage, printing beyond the quota, guest day passes, card replacements.
- **Payment is due by the 7th** of the month.
- **Pro-rata billing:** memberships starting on the 16th are billed pro-rata for the first month. Exits are billed up to the exit date.

## 2. GST
- **18% GST** is charged on all services (SAC 997212). GST is **not** charged on the security deposit.
- **Members with a GSTIN:** invoices carry their GSTIN so they can claim input tax credit. Member GSTINs must be Karnataka (state code 29) registrations, because services are supplied in Karnataka.
- **Members without a GSTIN** get a B2C invoice.
- **Changing the GSTIN, legal name or billing address:**
  - Email billing@ with the new GST registration certificate.
  - Changes received **by the 25th** apply from the next month's invoice.
  - An invoice already issued can be corrected **only if requested within 30 days of the invoice date and within the same financial year**. We do this by issuing a credit note and a re-issued invoice.
  - Older invoices cannot be changed.

## 3. TDS
- Members who must deduct TDS should deduct it **under Section 194-I at 10%** (rent for use of a building), **on the amount before GST**.
- We do **not** accept TDS deducted under Section 194C or 194J for seat fees.
- Members must share **Form 16A every quarter** within 15 days of the TDS return due date.
- TDS that does not show in our Form 26AS by the following quarter is treated as an **outstanding amount**.

## 4. Payment methods
- **Accepted:** NEFT, RTGS, IMPS or UPI to the bank account printed on the invoice, quoting the invoice number in the remarks.
- **Cheques are not accepted.**
- **Cards:** accepted only for day passes and event bookings, through the website.
- 🔒 **Our bank details never change by email.** If any email claims Launchbench's bank account has changed, please call your account manager. Likewise, **we never change a vendor's or member's bank account based on an email alone**. Changes need a signed letter on letterhead, a cancelled cheque, and a confirmation call to a phone number we already hold.

## 5. Late payment
| Days after the due date | What happens |
|---|---|
| After the 7th | A reminder email |
| Ongoing | A **late fee of 1.5% per month** on the overdue amount, added to the next invoice (GST applies to the late fee) |
| **Day 30** | A formal reminder and a **suspension warning** |
| **Day 45** | **Access cards suspended** until the dues are cleared. The deposit may be adjusted with notice. |

- **Waiving a late fee:** possible only once in 12 months, and only for members with a clean payment history. It needs the Finance Manager's approval.

## 6. Meeting-room overage and usage charges
- Each member's included meeting-room credits and actual usage are tracked monthly in `data/meeting_room_usage.csv`.
- **Overage is billed at the member rate:**
  - 4-seater: ₹480/hr
  - 8-seater: ₹800/hr
  - boardroom: ₹1,200/hr
- Bookings are logged in the member app, which also records who made each booking. If a member questions overage, send them their booking log for the month. Do not waive it unless there was a system error (for example, double-booking or a booking that was cancelled but still charged).

## 7. Disputes and corrections
- Raise any dispute **within 15 days of the invoice date** by emailing billing@ with the invoice number.
- **Pay the undisputed portion on time.** The disputed portion is put on hold, and **no late fee accrues on it** while under review.
- We aim to resolve disputes **within 5 business days**. If the member is right, we issue a **credit note** against the invoice. A refund is made only if no future invoices are expected, for example after an exit.
- **Invoices raised after an exit date in error** are cancelled in full by credit note. If such an invoice has been paid, the amount is refunded within 10 business days.

## 8. Invoice copies and statements
- Copies of any invoice, a statement of account, or a ledger confirmation are available on request to billing@ and are **sent within 1 business day**.
- Copies are sent **only to the member's registered billing contact**, or to an email address on the member's company domain.

## 9. Security deposit
- Deposits are refunded within 30 days of exit, after deductions. See [03-membership-terms.md §8](03-membership-terms.md).
- Refunds go only to the bank account registered at onboarding.
