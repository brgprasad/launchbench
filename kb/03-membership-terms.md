# Membership Terms (summary)

This is a plain-English summary of the Launchbench Membership Agreement. If anything here differs from the signed agreement, the signed agreement applies.

## 1. Starting a membership
- **What we need to start:**
  - a signed Membership Agreement
  - KYC: the company's PAN, its certificate of incorporation, and the GSTIN if the company has one
  - the security deposit
  - the first month's fee
- **Start dates:** memberships start on the 1st or the 16th of a month. Starting on the 16th means the first month is billed pro-rata.
- **Access cards:** one per seat, issued on day one.
- **Free trial:** leads of 5 or more seats can get a **1-day free trial** of their shortlisted space before signing.

## 2. Lock-in period
| Plan | Minimum lock-in | Optional longer lock-in |
|---|---|---|
| Hot desk | none (month-to-month) | 6 or 12 months for a discount |
| Dedicated desk | 3 months | 6 or 12 months |
| Founders Plan | 3 months | none (move to another plan instead) |
| Private cabin | 6 months | 12 months; longer only with Head of Sales approval |
| Virtual office | 12 months (prepaid) | none |

- During the lock-in, the member pays the full fee for the whole period, even if they leave early (see §5).
- **After the lock-in ends**, the membership continues **month-to-month** at the same price until either side gives notice, or until the member signs a new lock-in.

## 3. Annual price escalation
- For memberships that continue beyond 12 months, **seat fees rise by 6% on each anniversary of the contract start date**. A renewed 12-month lock-in keeps its 10% discount, applied to the escalated price.
- Add-on and usage prices follow the current [price list](02-pricing.md).

## 4. Notice period
| Plan | Notice required |
|---|---|
| Hot desk, dedicated desk, Founders Plan | **1 month** |
| Private cabin | **2 months** |
| Virtual office | none; it simply does not renew |

- Notice must be **given in writing by email to teamlaunchbench@gmail.com**, with the account manager in copy. Telling a community or reception staff member in person does not count.
- The notice period runs from the date we receive the email. The exit date is the later of:
  - the end of the notice period
  - the end of the lock-in
- We confirm every notice by email within 2 business days, stating the exit date and the final billing date.

## 5. Leaving early (during the lock-in)
- A member who wants to leave before the lock-in ends pays an **early-exit fee equal to the seat fees for the remaining lock-in months**.
- At the member's request, this fee can be adjusted against the security deposit.
- **Partial waiver:** if we re-let the same space to a new member before the lock-in ends, the Head of Sales may waive the remaining fees from the new member's start date onwards. This is decided case by case and is never promised in advance.

## 6. Changing seats
### Upgrading (adding seats, or moving to a bigger cabin)
- Allowed **at any time**, subject to availability.
- There is no fee.
- A new lock-in of the plan's minimum length starts on the upgrade date.
- The deposit is topped up to match the new plan.

### Downsizing during the lock-in
- **Desks:** one reduction of **up to 20% of seats** is allowed during the lock-in, with **2 months' notice**. Reductions beyond 20% are treated as a partial early exit: the early-exit fee (§5) applies to the extra seats released.
- **Cabins:** a cabin cannot be partly released. The team must move to a smaller cabin, if one is available. Moving to a smaller cabin during the lock-in counts as a partial early exit on the difference in seat fees for the remaining lock-in months.
- **Example:** a 20-seat cabin on a 12-month lock-in wants to move to a 12-seat cabin with 4 months of lock-in left.
  - The member gives 2 months' notice.
  - After the move, they pay the 12-seat fee.
  - They also pay the difference between the 20-seat and 12-seat fees for any lock-in months still remaining after the move.

### Downsizing after the lock-in
- Allowed with the normal notice period (§4) and no fee.

## 7. Moving between centres
- Allowed with **1 month's notice**, subject to availability. Prices change to the new centre's rates.
- The remaining lock-in carries over. If the new space has more seats, it counts as an upgrade (§6).

## 8. Security deposit refund
- Refunded **within 30 days of the exit date** to the member's registered bank account.
- **Deductions** can be made for:
  - unpaid invoices
  - usage charges
  - damage beyond normal wear and tear
  - unreturned access cards, at ₹500 each
- An itemised deduction statement is sent with the refund.

## 9. Suspension and termination by Launchbench
- If an invoice remains unpaid for **45 days**, access may be suspended after a written warning at day 30. See [04-billing-policy.md](04-billing-policy.md).
- Serious breaches of the [house rules](06-house-rules.md), such as harassment, illegal activity, or repeated safety violations, can lead to termination with immediate effect.

## 10. Assignment and subletting
- Members may not sublet or resell seats.
- **Group companies:** if the member's group companies use the space, they must be named in the agreement.
- **Acquisitions:** if the member company is acquired, the membership can be transferred to the acquirer with written consent.
