# Launchbench Centres

All 5 centres are in Bengaluru. Every centre has the following:
- **Internet:** 1 Gbps leased line with failover.
- **Power:** 100% power backup.
- **Pantry:** unlimited tea and coffee.
- **Phone booths and printing.**
- **Reception:** staffed 9 am–7 pm, Monday to Saturday.

### Opening hours (all centres)
| Who | Access |
|---|---|
| Hot desk members and day-pass users | 8 am–9 pm, Monday to Saturday. Closed Sundays and the public holidays listed below. |
| Dedicated desk, private cabin and Founders Plan members | 24×7 with an access card |
| Hot desk members with the 24×7 add-on | 24×7 with an access card |

**Public holidays (centres closed for hot desks and day passes):** Republic Day, Ugadi, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Deepavali (main day), Kannada Rajyotsava, Christmas.

**Tours:** Monday to Saturday, 10 am–6 pm, 30 minutes each. Book by emailing teamlaunchbench@gmail.com; the website's "Book a tour" button starts that email.
