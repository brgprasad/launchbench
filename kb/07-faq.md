# Frequently Asked Questions

All prices exclude 18% GST. The detailed policies are linked in each answer.

## Plans and pricing
**1. What is the cheapest way to work from Launchbench?**
A day pass costs ₹600. For regular use, a hot desk starts at ₹6,000/month at Hebbal and ₹6,500/month at Whitefield. See [02-pricing.md](02-pricing.md).

**2. What's the difference between a hot desk, a dedicated desk and a cabin?**
- **Hot desk:** any free seat in the open area, 8 am–9 pm, Monday to Saturday.
- **Dedicated desk:** your own fixed desk with a pedestal, available 24×7.
- **Private cabin:** a lockable room for your team only, 24×7. It is billed on its full capacity.

**3. We are a team of 14. How much is a cabin?**
Cabins come in fixed sizes, so a team of 14 takes a 16-seat cabin. On a 12-month lock-in that costs:

| Centre | Monthly fee |
|---|---|
| Whitefield | ₹1,44,000 |
| Hebbal | ₹1,36,800 |
| HSR Layout | ₹1,72,800 |
| Koramangala | ₹1,94,400 |
| Indiranagar | ₹2,01,600 |

Check `availability.csv` for open cabins.

**4. Do you offer discounts?**
Yes:
- 5% off seat fees for a 6-month lock-in.
- 10% off for a 12-month lock-in.
- 15% off for the first 6 months for startups referred by our partners: Pragati Launch Accelerator, Kaveri Seed Partners and Saffron Angels Network. The partner discount does not stack with the lock-in discount; the higher one applies.

**5. What is the Founders Plan?**
1 to 3 dedicated desks at ₹9,000 per seat per month, with a 3-month lock-in and 10 meeting-room credits per month for the team. It is for companies incorporated less than 3 years ago, or DPIIT-recognised startups. It is offered only at Koramangala, Indiranagar and HSR Layout.

**6. Can I try before I commit?**
- Teams of 5 or more seats get a free 1-day trial.
- Anyone can buy a ₹600 day pass.
- Tours run Monday to Saturday, 10 am–6 pm.

**7. Is there a security deposit?**
Yes, and it is refundable:

| Plan | Deposit (of the monthly seat fee) |
|---|---|
| Hot desk, Founders Plan | 1 month |
| Dedicated desk, cabin | 2 months |

No GST is charged on deposits. See [03-membership-terms.md](03-membership-terms.md).

**8. Is there a minimum commitment?**
| Plan | Minimum commitment |
|---|---|
| Hot desk | none (month-to-month) |
| Dedicated desk | 3 months |
| Founders Plan | 3 months |
| Cabin | 6 months |

**9. Do prices go up?**
Seat fees rise by 6% on each contract anniversary, for memberships that continue beyond 12 months.

## Meeting rooms and events
**10. How many meeting-room hours do I get?**
1 credit equals 1 hour in a 4- or 8-seater room. The boardroom uses 2 credits per hour.

| Plan | Credits per month |
|---|---|
| Hot desk | 2 per seat |
| Dedicated desk, cabin | 4 per seat |
| Founders Plan | 10 for the team |

Credits don't roll over.

**11. What if I use more than my credits?**
Extra hours are billed on your next invoice at the member rate:

| Room | Member rate |
|---|---|
| 4-seater | ₹480/hr |
| 8-seater | ₹800/hr |
| Boardroom | ₹1,200/hr |

**12. Can non-members book meeting rooms?**
Yes, at list rates:

| Room | List rate |
|---|---|
| 4-seater | ₹600/hr |
| 8-seater | ₹1,000/hr |
| Boardroom | ₹1,500/hr |

**13. Can we host a launch or demo day?**
Yes, in our event halls:

| Event hall | Half day | Full day |
|---|---|---|
| Koramangala (80 people) | ₹22,000 | ₹40,000 |
| Whitefield (120 people) | ₹26,000 | ₹48,000 |

- Members get 20% off.
- A 50% advance is needed to confirm.
- Catering is quoted separately.

## Access, guests and amenities
**14. Can hot desk members get 24×7 access?**
Yes, for ₹1,500/month extra. Dedicated desk, cabin and Founders Plan members get 24×7 access free.

**15. Can I bring guests?**
- Meeting guests are free.
- A guest who will work at a desk needs a ₹400 guest day pass, billed to you.
- Up to 4 visitors per member at a time in common areas.

**16. Can I bring my dog?**
Only at **HSR Layout**, in the ground-floor Paws Zone, Monday to Friday. Dogs must be vaccinated and leashed, and the owner signs a waiver. Pets are not allowed at our other centres. See [06-house-rules.md](06-house-rules.md).

**17. Is there parking?**
Yes, at every centre:

| Centres | Car parking |
|---|---|
| Koramangala, Indiranagar | ₹4,000/month (Indiranagar has a waitlist) |
| HSR Layout, Whitefield, Hebbal | ₹3,000/month |

Two-wheeler parking is ₹800/month everywhere.

**18. Which centres are near a metro station?**
| Centre | Nearest station |
|---|---|
| Indiranagar | Indiranagar station, about 600 m |
| Whitefield | Kadugodi Tree Park, about 800 m |

Hebbal runs a free shuttle from the Hebbal flyover at peak hours.

**19. Do you accept couriers on our behalf?**
- Reception accepts couriers 9 am–7 pm, Monday to Saturday.
- Items are held for 7 days.
- Tell reception a day ahead for deliveries over 20 kg.
- No cash-on-delivery parcels.

**20. Can we get a static IP or a private network?**
A private VLAN for cabins is free. A static public IP costs ₹1,500/month.

**21. Is there food?**
- Unlimited tea and coffee at every centre.
- The Burn Rate Café at Koramangala serves meals from 8 am to 8 pm.

## Billing
**22. When are invoices raised and when are they due?**
Invoices are raised on the 1st and due on the 7th. Seat fees are billed in advance; usage such as meeting-room overage is billed in arrears. See [04-billing-policy.md](04-billing-policy.md).

**23. Can we deduct TDS?**
Yes, under Section 194-I at 10%, on the amount before GST. Please share Form 16A every quarter.

**24. We need to change the GSTIN on our invoices.**
Email billing@ with the GST certificate:
- By the 25th, the change applies from next month's invoice.
- Invoices issued in the last 30 days, in the same financial year, can be corrected with a credit note and a re-issued invoice.

**25. What happens if we pay late?**
- A late fee of 1.5% per month is charged.
- At day 30 we send a suspension warning.
- At day 45 access is suspended.

**26. How do we pay?**
By NEFT, RTGS, IMPS or UPI to the account on the invoice. Cheques are not accepted. Our bank details never change by email.

## Changes and exits
**27. How do we cancel?**
Email teamlaunchbench@gmail.com with your account manager in copy:
- Desks need 1 month's notice.
- Cabins need 2 months' notice.

You cannot exit before your lock-in ends unless you pay the early-exit fee.

**28. Can we downsize?**
- **After the lock-in:** yes, with normal notice.
- **During the lock-in, desks:** one reduction of up to 20% of seats is allowed, with 2 months' notice.
- **During the lock-in, cabins:** you must move to a smaller cabin, and the fee difference for the remaining lock-in is payable.

See [03-membership-terms.md §6](03-membership-terms.md).

**29. Can we upgrade or add seats?**
Yes, at any time, subject to availability, with no fee. A new minimum lock-in starts from the upgrade date.

**30. When do we get our deposit back?**
Within 30 days of the exit date, after any deductions for dues, damage or unreturned cards (₹500 each).
