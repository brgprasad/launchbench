## What we do
Launchbench runs flexible, managed workspaces for **startups (seed stage to Series B)**, SMEs, and satellite teams of larger companies. We sell:
- **Hot desks**: any open seat, month-to-month.
- **Dedicated desks**: your own fixed desk, 24×7 access.
- **Private cabins**: lockable team rooms for 4 to 30 people.
- **Founders Plan**: a low-commitment dedicated-desk plan for very early teams of 1 to 3 people.
- **Meeting rooms and event halls**, bookable by members and non-members.
- **Day passes** and **virtual offices** (business address, mail handling, GST registration documents).

Every centre includes high-speed internet, power backup, housekeeping, a pantry with unlimited tea and coffee, phone booths, printing, and a community team.

Our meeting rooms are named after funding stages: **Pre-Seed** and **Seed** (4-seaters), **Series A** and **Series B** (8-seaters), and **IPO** (the boardroom). Our café is the **Burn Rate Café**.

## Community and startup programme
- **Partner programme:** startups referred by our partner accelerators and funds get **15% off seat fees for their first 6 months**. Current partners are Pragati Launch Accelerator, Kaveri Seed Partners and Saffron Angels Network. Details are in [02-pricing.md](02-pricing.md).
- **Demo Night:** on the last Friday of each month, a member startup pitches to the community at the Koramangala event hall. Members attend free.
- **Office hours:** mentors from partner funds hold drop-in sessions every 2nd Wednesday at Koramangala and Whitefield.

## Escalation ladder
1. Centre Manager (for facility issues) or Account Manager (for commercial issues)
2. Head of Operations (facilities) or Head of Sales (commercial) or Finance Manager (money)
3. COO (Meera Iyer)

Escalate straight to step 2 when any of these applies:
- the member threatens to leave or mentions legal action
- the same issue has been reported 3 or more times in 30 days
- an SLA has already been breached
- the matter involves safety, security, or suspected fraud

## Customer-facing tone
- Warm, concise and practical. Address people by first name, sign off as "Team Launchbench".
- Never promise a discount, waiver, or credit that is not written in these policies. Say the request has been passed to the right person instead.
- Never confirm bank-account changes, refunds, or contract amendments by email alone.
- Quote prices **excluding GST** and always state "+ 18% GST".
