# Facility Support and Service Levels (SLA)

Members report issues to **teamlaunchbench@gmail.com**, in the member app, or at reception.
- **support@ hours:** monitored **8 am–10 pm, every day**.
- **P1 emergencies:** also reachable 24×7 on the centre's emergency line, which is printed on every access card and in the app.

Every issue becomes a ticket, logged in `data/tickets.csv`, with a priority, an owner and SLA due times. The owner is normally the **centre manager** of the member's centre, and the facilities team does the work.

## Priority levels
| Priority | Definition | Examples | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Critical** | A safety or security risk, or **a whole centre, floor or cabin unable to work** | Internet down for a floor or the centre; power out beyond backup; water leaking onto desks or equipment; fire alarm fault; someone stuck in a lift; security breach or theft | **30 minutes** (24×7) | **4 hours** |
| **P2 High** | A serious problem affecting **one cabin or team**, or a shared facility down | AC not working in a cabin; Wi-Fi or LAN down in one cabin; a member's access card system not working; no drinking water on a floor; washroom out of order | **2 hours** | **1 business day (24 hrs)** |
| **P3 Medium** | Inconvenient but work can continue | Broken chair; light fused; printer jammed or out of toner; housekeeping missed a cabin; slow Wi-Fi at one desk; noisy neighbour | **1 business day** | **3 business days** |
| **P4 Low / request** | Requests and improvements | Extra furniture; nameplate or signage; new access card; whiteboard; plant; feature suggestions | **2 business days** | **7 business days** |

**How to set the priority**
1. Choose the **higher** priority when unsure.
2. **Raise the priority by one level** in either of these cases:
   - **The same issue is reported again** after being marked resolved, within 30 days.
   - **The resolution target has already passed.** For example, a P2 still open after 24 hours is handled as a P1.
3. **Health, safety and security issues are always P1**, however small they seem.

## Escalation ladder
| Stage | When | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal handling | Centre Manager, through the facilities team |
| 2 | Resolution target missed, a repeat issue (3+ times in 30 days), or the member is angry or threatens to leave | **Head of Operations (Kavya Reddy)**, plus the member's account manager in copy |
| 3 | Escalated issue unresolved after another 24 hours, any legal threat, or any injury | **COO (Meera Iyer)** |

## Service credits
- **P1 incidents:** if a P1 incident **stops a member's space from being used for more than 24 hours**, the member gets a **pro-rata service credit** of the affected seats' fee for each full day lost.
- **P2 incidents:** if a P2 issue in a cabin stays unresolved for **more than 3 business days**, the member gets a **10% credit on that month's fee for that cabin**.
- **Applying credits:** credits go on the next invoice. The centre manager must approve them, and the member must request them within 30 days.
- **What the AI assistant may say about credits:** it may tell a member that they *may be eligible* for a credit under this policy. It must **never confirm** a credit amount.

## What the first response must contain
1. An acknowledgement of the issue, in the member's own words.
2. The ticket number and the priority.
3. Who is handling it, and the resolution target in clock time. For example: "We aim to fix this by 4 pm tomorrow."
4. A workaround where one exists. For example: "A spare meeting room (Series B) is free today if your team needs to move."
5. For a repeat or overdue issue: an apology and a statement that it has been escalated, naming the person.

## Planned maintenance
