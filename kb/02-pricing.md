# Launchbench Pricing (effective 1 April 2026)

- All prices are in **Indian Rupees (₹), excluding 18% GST** (SAC 997212).
- Prices are **per seat per month** unless stated otherwise.
- The machine-readable source of these prices is `data/pricing.csv`. If anything differs, the CSV is correct.

## 2. Lock-in discounts
| Lock-in | Discount on seat fees |
|---|---|
| Month-to-month (hot desk only) | 0% |
| 3 months | 0% |
| 6 months | **5%** |
| 12 months | **10%** |

- The discount applies to **seat fees only**, not to add-ons, usage, meeting rooms or events.
- **Partner programme:** startups referred by Pragati Launch Accelerator, Kaveri Seed Partners or Saffron Angels Network get **15% off seat fees for the first 6 months**. It does **not** stack with the lock-in discount. The higher of the two applies in each month. The member must send a referral email from the partner, or show their cohort or portfolio listing.

## 3. Founders Plan
For very early teams that are not ready for a long commitment.
- **Seats:** 1 to 3 dedicated desks, at ₹9,000 per seat per month.
- **Lock-in and deposit:** 3-month lock-in, 1-month security deposit.
- **Included:** 24×7 access, **10 meeting-room credits per month for the whole team** (not per seat), and free entry to Demo Night and office hours.
- **Eligibility:** the company must be **incorporated less than 3 years ago** (we need the certificate of incorporation) **or** be DPIIT-recognised. Sole proprietors and freelancers are not eligible.
- **Moving on:** a team that grows beyond 3 seats moves to regular dedicated desks or a cabin, and the new plan's lock-in starts from the date of the move.

## 4. Meeting-room credits included every month
| Plan | Credits included |
|---|---|
| Hot desk | 2 per seat |
| Dedicated desk | 4 per seat |
| Private cabin | 4 per seat of cabin capacity |
| Founders Plan | 10 for the whole team |
| Virtual office | none |

- **How credits are used:**
  - 1 credit = 1 hour in a 4-seater or 8-seater room.
  - The boardroom (12-seater) uses **2 credits per hour**.
  - Event halls cannot be paid for with credits.
- **Credits do not roll over** to the next month and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- **Hours beyond the included credits are billed at the member rate**, which is list price less 20%. Overage is billed in arrears on the next month's invoice.

- Use of the centre's address as your business address.
- Mail and courier receipt, with scanned copies on request and forwarding once a fortnight.

The GST Registration plan also includes the documents needed for GST and MCA registration: NOC, utility bill and rent agreement.

## 9. Worked examples (excluding GST unless stated)
1. **16-seat cabin at Whitefield on a 12-month lock-in**
   - Seat fee: 16 × ₹10,000 = ₹1,60,000, less 10% = **₹1,44,000/month**
   - GST: ₹25,920, so ₹1,69,920 per month including GST
   - Deposit: 2 × ₹1,44,000 = ₹2,88,000
   - Meeting-room credits: 64 per month
2. **16-seat cabin at Indiranagar on a 12-month lock-in**
   - Seat fee: 16 × ₹14,000 = ₹2,24,000, less 10% = **₹2,01,600/month**
3. **3 hot desks at Koramangala, month-to-month**
   - Seat fee: 3 × ₹8,500 = **₹25,500/month**
   - Deposit: ₹25,500
   - Meeting-room credits: 6 per month
4. **Founders Plan, 2 seats at HSR Layout**
   - Seat fee: 2 × ₹9,000 = **₹18,000/month**
   - Deposit: ₹18,000
   - Lock-in: 3 months
5. **Partner-referred 8-seat cabin at HSR on a 12-month lock-in**
   - Months 1–6: the partner discount of 15% beats the lock-in discount of 10%. 8 × ₹12,000 = ₹96,000, less 15% = **₹81,600/month**
   - Months 7–12: the lock-in discount of 10% applies. ₹96,000 less 10% = **₹86,400/month**
   - Deposit: 2 × ₹86,400 = ₹1,72,800 (based on the regular discounted fee)

## 10. What sales staff can and cannot offer
- **Sales staff can offer:** the prices and discounts on this page, a free 1-day trial for leads of 5 or more seats, and no security deposit on a Founders Plan for partner-referred startups.
- **Head of Sales approval is needed for:**
  - any other discount
  - lock-ins longer than 12 months, which can carry up to 15% off
  - custom fit-outs
  - teams of more than 40 seats
