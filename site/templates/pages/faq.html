Plans and pricing

{% call qa('What is the cheapest way to work from Launchbench?', open=true) %}

A day pass is {{ prices['DAY-ALL']|inr }}. For regular use, hot desks start at {{ centre_by_code['HEB'].price_hot|inr }} a month at Hebbal and {{ centre_by_code['WHF'].price_hot|inr }} at Whitefield.

{% endcall %} {% call qa("What's the difference between a hot desk, a dedicated desk and a cabin?") %}

Hot desk: any free seat in the open area, 8 am to 9 pm, Monday to Saturday. Dedicated desk: your own fixed desk with a pedestal, 24×7. Private cabin: a lockable room for your team only, 24×7, billed on its full capacity.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('We are a team of 14. How much is a cabin?') %}

Cabins come in fixed sizes, so a team of 14 takes a 16-seat cabin. On a 12-month lock-in, that costs per month:

  • {{ ctr.name.split(' (')[0] }}{{ (16 * ctr.price_cab * 0.9)|inr }}
    • {% endfor %}

See each centre's page for cabins opening soon.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Do you offer discounts?') %}

Yes. {{ prices['DISC-LOCKIN-6'] }}% off seat fees for a 6-month lock-in, and {{ prices['DISC-LOCKIN-12'] }}% for 12 months. Startups referred by our partners ({{ c.partners|join(', ') }}) get {{ prices['DISC-PARTNER'] }}% off for their first 6 months. The partner discount doesn't stack with a lock-in discount; the higher one applies.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('What is the Founders Plan?') %}

1 to 3 dedicated desks at {{ from_fnd|inr }} per seat per month, with a 3-month lock-in and 10 meeting-room credits a month for the team. It's for companies incorporated less than 3 years ago, or DPIIT-recognised startups, at Koramangala, Indiranagar and HSR Layout. More about the Founders Plan

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can I try before I commit?') %}

Teams of 5 or more seats get a free 1-day trial. Anyone can buy a {{ prices['DAY-ALL']|inr }} day pass. And tours run Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Is there a security deposit?') %}

Yes, and it's refundable and interest-free: 1 month of seat fees for hot desks and the Founders Plan, 2 months for dedicated desks and cabins. No GST is charged on deposits.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Is there a minimum commitment?') %}

Hot desks are month-to-month. Dedicated desks and the Founders Plan have a 3-month minimum, and private cabins 6 months. After the lock-in, your membership simply continues month-to-month.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Do prices go up?') %}

For memberships that continue beyond 12 months, seat fees rise by 6% on each contract anniversary.

{% endcall %}

Meeting rooms and events

{% call qa('How many meeting-room hours do I get?') %}

Hot desks get 2 credits per seat each month, dedicated desks and cabins 4 per seat, and the Founders Plan 10 for the team. 1 credit is 1 hour in a 4- or 8-seater; the boardroom uses 2 credits an hour. Credits don't roll over.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('What if I use more than my credits?') %}

Extra hours go on your next invoice at the member rate: {{ (prices['MR-4'] * 0.8)|inr }}/hr for a 4-seater, {{ (prices['MR-8'] * 0.8)|inr }}/hr for an 8-seater and {{ (prices['MR-12'] * 0.8)|inr }}/hr for the boardroom.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can non-members book meeting rooms?') %}

Yes, at list rates: {{ prices['MR-4']|inr }}/hr for a 4-seater, {{ prices['MR-8']|inr }}/hr for an 8-seater and {{ prices['MR-12']|inr }}/hr for the boardroom.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can we host a launch or demo day?') %}

Yes. Our Koramangala hall seats 80 ({{ prices['EVT-KOR-HALF']|inr }} half day, {{ prices['EVT-KOR-FULL']|inr }} full day) and Whitefield seats 120 ({{ prices['EVT-WHF-HALF']|inr }} / {{ prices['EVT-WHF-FULL']|inr }}). Members get 20% off. A 50% advance confirms the booking, and catering is quoted separately. About the event halls

Access, guests and amenities

{% call qa('Can hot desk members get 24×7 access?') %}

Yes, for {{ prices['ACCESS247-HOT']|inr }} a month extra. Dedicated desk, cabin and Founders Plan members get 24×7 access at no extra cost.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can I bring guests?') %}

Meeting guests are free. A guest who'll work at a desk needs a {{ prices['GUEST-ALL']|inr }} guest day pass, billed to you. Up to 4 visitors per member at a time in common areas.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can I bring my dog?') %}

Only at HSR Layout, in the ground-floor Paws Zone, Monday to Friday. Dogs must be vaccinated and leashed, and the owner signs a waiver. Pets aren't allowed at our other centres, except assistance animals.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Is there parking?') %}

Yes, at every centre. Car parking is {{ prices['PARK-CAR-KOR']|inr }} a month at Koramangala and Indiranagar (Indiranagar has a waitlist), and {{ prices['PARK-CAR-HSR']|inr }} at HSR Layout, Whitefield and Hebbal. Two-wheeler parking is {{ prices['PARK-BIKE-ALL']|inr }} a month everywhere.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Which centres are near a metro station?') %}

Indiranagar is about 600 m from Indiranagar station, and Whitefield about 800 m from Kadugodi Tree Park. Hebbal runs a free shuttle from the Hebbal flyover bus stop at peak hours on weekdays.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Do you accept couriers on our behalf?') %}

Reception accepts couriers 9 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday, and holds them for 7 days. Let reception know a day ahead for anything over 20 kg. We can't accept cash-on-delivery parcels.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can we get a static IP or a private network?') %}

A private VLAN for cabins is free. A static public IP is {{ prices['STATICIP-ALL']|inr }} a month.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Is there food?') %}

Unlimited tea and coffee at every centre, and the Burn Rate Café at Koramangala serves meals from 8 am to 8 pm.

{% endcall %}

Billing

{% call qa('When are invoices raised and when are they due?') %}

Invoices go out on the 1st and are due by the 7th. Seat fees are billed in advance; usage such as meeting-room overage is billed in arrears.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can we deduct TDS?') %}

Yes, under Section 194-I at 10%, on the amount before GST. Please share Form 16A every quarter.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('How do we pay?') %}

By NEFT, RTGS, IMPS or UPI to the account printed on the invoice. We don't accept cheques. Our bank details never change by email. If you ever receive an email saying they have, call your account manager before paying.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('What happens if we pay late?') %}

A late fee of 1.5% a month applies. At 30 days we send a suspension warning, and at 45 days access is suspended.

{% endcall %}

Changes and exits

{% call qa('Can we upgrade or add seats?') %}

Yes, at any time and subject to availability, with no fee. A new minimum lock-in starts from the upgrade date.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('Can we downsize?') %}

After your lock-in, yes, with normal notice. During the lock-in, desk members can make one reduction of up to 20% of seats with 2 months' notice. Cabin teams move to a smaller cabin, and pay the fee difference for the rest of the lock-in.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('How do we cancel?') %}

Send us notice in writing by email, with your account manager in copy. Desks need 1 month's notice and cabins 2 months. Leaving before the end of a lock-in means paying the seat fees for the remaining months.

{% endcall %} {% call qa('When do we get our deposit back?') %}

Within 30 days of your exit date, less any dues, damage or unreturned access cards ({{ prices['CARD-REPLACE']|inr }} each).
